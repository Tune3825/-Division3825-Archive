DIVISION 3825 // SHADOW DIRECTIVE
SPACIE

The Brother in the Shadows

Spacie is Tune's younger brother, a Cycle-aware tactician and the leader of the Shadow Directive.

ArchiveCharactersDivision StructureFounder
Role: Shadow Directive leader
Relationship: Tune's younger brother
Methods: rift control, covert intervention, mission manipulation
Signatures: red-and-black rose or sunflower

The master canon places Spacie behind many interventions Tune does not initially understand. He remembers more of the repeating Cycle than Tune does and acts to preserve the Founder's ability to choose rather than simply forcing outcomes.

The Shadow Directive operates beneath ordinary Division logs. In older World Core records, its work includes sealing Cycle-break anomalies, suppressing dangerous Hybrid events and redirecting dimensional access.

Recognition marker: flowers are repeatedly used as evidence of Spacie's unseen intervention.
