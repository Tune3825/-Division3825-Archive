ENIGMA

The Anchor

Enigma is Tune's closest tactical partner and the grounding presence that keeps emotional and dimensional instability from consuming him.

ArchiveCharactersFounderSystems
Role: tactical partner / anchor
Combat: adaptive pistol and Sunsteel Blade
Function: precision, grounding, tactical stability
Relationship: loyalty earned through shared survival

The master canon defines Enigma as the balance to Tune's emotional volatility. She is action-oriented, pragmatic and capable in her own right, with her partnership to Tune built through survival rather than destiny.

Her presence is repeatedly associated with Tune's stability and with SEA / Prime Echo activation conditions.

Canon principle: Enigma is not a sidekick; she is the balancing anchor of the core team.
