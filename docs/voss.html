FRANK VOSS

The Keeper

Frank Voss is a former Division 3825 commander transformed into the Keeper of the Ledger and one of the principal antagonists of the stabilized Cycle.

ArchiveCharactersBlack LedgerErevos
Role: Keeper of the Ledger
Former role: Division 3825 commander
Capabilities: Ledger rewriting, chain manipulation, Fractured summoning, dimensional locking
Persistence: tied to Ledger imprint

Voss represents the Ledger's enforcement layer: precise, controlled and persistent. The master canon treats his continued existence as a function of what the Ledger has written rather than ordinary biological immortality.

Threat rule: destroying the body is not the same as erasing the page.
