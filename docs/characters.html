People of the Cycle

The Tuneverse centers on identity, memory, recurrence and the people who keep the Founder from facing the Cycle alone.

Tune — The Founder

Human Mutacore wielder, Founder of Division 3825, brother of Spacie and anchor of the recurring Cycle. His arc runs through identity, burden, revelation and the attempt to change recursion rather than merely survive it.

Spacie

Nyx — The Oracle

A high-ranking Division 3825 guide with partial Cycle-awareness, predictive intuition and dimensional insight. Nyx challenges Tune toward truth rather than controlling him.

Enigma — The Anchor

Erevos — The Anti-Founder

The inverse of the Founder: a Ledger-aligned ascendant born from Architect remnants, digital survival logic and Omnishade influence. Erevos seeks order through rewriting rather than choice.

The Wanderer

The Ledger Council's Fourth Seat and a chain-linked anomaly bound to Tune. The connection transfers memory echoes, pain and vulnerability across layers.

Shade

A Phantom operative associated with deep-cover Fractured territory. His status is uncertain inside the older World Core archive, where rumors place him beyond ordinary Division reach.

Canon principle: the Fractured are not separate monsters. They are broken outcomes and distorted possibilities of Tune.
