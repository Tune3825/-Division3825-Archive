AUTHORITATIVE SPINE

The Cycle Order

The later Canon Spine supersedes older timeline summaries where they conflict. Failure comes first; stability comes later.

ArchiveCycle ZeroBooksCanon Rules
One-line spine: The Tuneverse begins with a failed first cycle. Everything that follows exists to correct it.

1. Cycle Zero — The First Cycle

Status: Failed.

The first full execution of the Cycle. Systems and names exist but have not yet become stable or binding. A Fractured breach occurs too early, the structure cannot hold, and reality performs a hard reset.

The Founder's Flame — Internal Record

Direct participation in Cycle Zero: early system emergence, early identities, ignition, escalation and the conditions that cause the first cycle to fail.

The Anomaly — External Residue

The scar left after Cycle Zero collapses. Reality detects pressure and recurrence without possessing stable authority or complete context.

2. The Founder's Protocol — Later Cycle

Status: First stabilized cycle.

Systems mature, naming becomes binding, authority coheres, Division 3825 forms and memory begins to hold across repetition. Protocol is not the first Cycle; it is the first successful stabilization.

3. The Black Ledger — Truth Weaponization

Status: Post-stabilization escalation.

Truth becomes enforceable. Records can overwrite earlier cycles, names can alter reality retroactively and failed cycles become vulnerable to rewriting, erasure or repurposing.

Core continuity rules

Cycle Zero is the first attempt, not pre-history.
Failure precedes stability.
Names exist before they bind.
Systems exist before they hold.
Protocol is first successful, not first chronologically.
Truth is imposed later rather than native to reality.
