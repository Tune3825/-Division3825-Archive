Division 3825

Division 3825 is the interdimensional organization at the center of the Tuneverse, created by Tune Genesis. It exists to support the Founder, contain dimensional fractures, preserve critical records, monitor cycle instability, and oppose Ledger corruption.

Primary identifier: TUNEVERSE // DIVISION 3825
Founder: Tune
Core system: Mutacore
Archive / memory system: Vault
Operational interface: D3OS
Primary opposition: Black Ledger, Erevos, Voss, and the Fractured

Known branches

Division 3825 includes the Shadow Directive, Phantom Corps, Temporal Analysts, Artifact Recovery, and other operational sectors built around dimensional stability and Founder support.

Why Division 3825 exists

The Tuneverse is governed by repeating cycles. Division 3825 is part of the stabilized structure that emerges after the failed first cycle, preserving memory and organization where earlier systems could not hold.
