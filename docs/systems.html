CORE SYSTEMS

How the Tuneverse Works

The Tuneverse is governed by identity, memory, truth, corruption and resonance rather than ordinary physics alone.

ArchiveMutacoreLedgerStyles

Mutacore

A bio-quantum identity engine bound to Tune. It expresses Styles, responds to resonance, can synthesize Hybrids and reflects possible selves across the Cycle.

The Vault

The preservation system: Founder logs, Style templates, dimensional archives and immutable memory. The Vault recognizes Tune even when Tune does not recognize himself.

The Black Ledger

The anti-Vault. It weaponizes names, rewrites truth and preserves corrupted identities through pages, chains and hierarchy.

Chain System

Metaphysical tethers across dimensions, Cycles and layers. Chains can carry memory, pain, energy and access. They can be overwritten or redirected, but not simply broken.

SEA / Prime Echo

Tune's unique situational defense. The World Core describes SEA as changing how danger and injury are experienced rather than functioning as ordinary physical armor.

Resonance

The synchronization between Tune's identity and a Style template. Emotional clarity and dimensional alignment can strengthen it; overload can trigger unstable events.

Hybrids

Temporary collisions between two Style identities under extreme pressure. Known examples include NightRage, Syncfire, Chronotide and MOBLINE.

Variants

Non-template transformations caused by Mutacore stress or emotional overload. They are neither normal Styles nor true Hybrids.
