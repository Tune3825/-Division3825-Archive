DIVISION 3825 // THREAT ARCHIVE

The Black Ledger

The Black Ledger is the anti-Vault of the Tuneverse. Where the Vault preserves memory and truth, the Ledger weaponizes names, rewrites identity, and enforces corrupted reality.

Erevos stands as the Anti-Founder. Frank Voss serves as Keeper. Fractured entities persist through Ledger pages, making the conflict with Division 3825 a battle over memory, authority, identity, and what reality is allowed to remember.

The Black Ledger is also the post-stabilization Tuneverse novel in which truth itself becomes a weapon.
