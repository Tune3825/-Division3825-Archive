Mutacore Styles

Styles are identity expressions generated through the Mutacore. Each represents a possible version of Tune aligned to a distinct dimensional and emotional combat logic.

ArchiveMutacoreFracturedSystems

Lockrage

Shooter Style. Tactical red/black/gold combat expression centered on firearms, pressure and direct survival.

Nightblood

Horror Style. Gothic, shadow-heavy expression built around fear, predation and nightmare logic.

Redline

Racing Style. Kinetic driver expression built around velocity, momentum, control and the Redline vehicle.

Speedsurge

Speedster Style. Extreme movement, red-gold lightning and acceleration independent of Redline's racing identity.

Voltide

Nuclear Style. Radiation, reactor-scale force and dangerous containment pressure.

Chronix

Wizard / temporal Style. Runes, time manipulation and ancient-energy control.

Veilwalker

Spy Style. Stealth, holographic deception, suppressed weapons and infiltration.

Nightfall

Assassin Style. Parkour, precision, blades and shadowed traversal.

Vyrex

Cyberpunk Style. Predictive systems, neon glitch logic and machine-linked combat.

Exotrix

Sci-fi super-soldier Style. Exoskeleton systems, tactical holography and particle weaponry.

Pnuembra

Fable Style. Mythic, narrative and storybook reality expression.

Blitzfire

Stylized high-chaos combat Style associated with bright arena logic and weapon improvisation.

Rule: Styles are not Hybrids. Hybrids are temporary collisions between Style identities and remain unstable.
