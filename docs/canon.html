IMMUTABLE RULES

Canon Laws

These rules summarize the fixed metaphysical and continuity constraints identified by the master canon. The later Canon Spine controls Cycle ordering where older material differs.

1. Tune is the Founder.
2. The Mutacore is bound to Tune's identity rather than functioning as an ordinary interchangeable device.
3. The Fractured are distorted versions and outcomes of Tune.
4. Spacie is Tune's younger brother and repeatedly intervenes across Cycles.
5. The Ledger is the corrupting system; the Fractured are caught within its persistence.
6. Erevos is the Anti-Founder.
7. The Vault recognizes Tune across memory loss and recurrence.
8. SEA / Prime Echo belongs uniquely to Tune.
9. Hybrids are not permanent Styles; they are unstable collisions.
10. The Cycle cannot be escaped by simple force. It changes only when the Founder changes.
11. Standard state is the baseline outside active conflict; transformed states resolve back toward it.
12. Chains can be overwritten, redirected or rebound, but are not casually severed.
13. Nyx guides the Founder; she is not his keeper.
14. Enigma functions as Tune's grounding balance and tactical anchor.
15. The novels remain the core continuity for adaptations and extensions.

Authoritative Cycle rule

Cycle Zero is the failed first cycle. The Founder's Protocol is the first stabilized later cycle. The Black Ledger follows stability by weaponizing truth, names and records.
