EREVOS

The Anti-Founder

Erevos is the structural inverse of Tune: the Anti-Founder who turns identity, naming and memory into instruments of imposed order.

The older master canon frames Erevos as born from the collapse of the Convergence Architect and aligned with the logic that becomes the mature Ledger system. In the stabilized era, he stands opposite the Founder not merely as an enemy but as a competing answer to what the Cycle should become.

Canon role: every stable Cycle ultimately generates both a Founder and an Anti-Founder.
