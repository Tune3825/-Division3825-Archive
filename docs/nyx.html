NYX

The Oracle

Nyx is a high-ranking Division 3825 guide whose role is continuity, warning and truth—not control.

ArchiveCharactersTimelineCycle Zero
Role: guide / tactical analyst
Awareness: partial Cycle knowledge
Strengths: predictive intuition, dimensional insight, recon
Function: steer Tune toward truth without taking away choice

Across the canon material, Nyx recognizes recurrence before Tune fully understands it. Her warnings carry the weight of remembered failure, and her relationship to the Founder is defined by guidance rather than ownership.

The Anomaly places Nyx at the center of Cycle Zero's external residue and the early conceptual architecture that later becomes Division 3825.

Canon principle: Nyx is Tune's guide, not his keeper.
